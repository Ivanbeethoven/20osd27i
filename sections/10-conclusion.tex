\section{Conclusion}

\sys organizes three optional capabilities over a common file-system and
object-storage path. Delta-based workspaces compose a shared baseline with
private changes. Packed metadata provides indexed access to frozen records
for standalone read-only views or workspace lowers. Variable-granularity
object storage separates client object formation at $g$ from backend
encoding and physical verification at $(B,q)$. Each capability can be enabled
for its own purpose; none requires the other two as an architectural
prerequisite. The planned evaluation will compare supported configurations
under matched contracts to determine their individual and combined costs,
their effects on complete agent execution, and where existing approaches
remain preferable. Composition does not imply an additional gain, and no
quantitative performance conclusion is drawn in this draft.

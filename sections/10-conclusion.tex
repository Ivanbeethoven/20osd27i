\section{Conclusion}

\sys separates the representation of private workspace changes from
the service of shared metadata and the layout of stored bytes.
Range mappings preserve unchanged data, authenticated joint pages
serve published read-only metadata outside the mutable database, and
a co-designed RustFS interface carries object-layout decisions through
upload, read, and repair. These mechanisms remain independently
selectable, with preparation and access costs appropriate to different
workloads. The implementation and eight-run request characterization
provide the basis for evaluating their effects on workspace creation,
repeated access, and worker handoff.

\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:evaluation}

Explain the research questions, workloads or datasets, baselines, metrics, and
experimental procedure before presenting results. Report enough information
for the reader to judge the validity and limits of the conclusions. Tie each
claim to a table, figure, or statistical test, and separate measured results
from hypotheses or engineering expectations.

\subsection{Experimental Setup}
Describe hardware, software versions, parameter choices, and how randomness
was controlled. Put long configuration details in an appendix only when the
conference policy allows it.

\subsection{Results}
Replace this text with the main results. A short paragraph should explain the
most important trend in each table or figure and why it answers the relevant
research question.

\subsection{Limitations}
State the cases your evaluation does not cover and how those cases affect the
scope of the claims.

\section{Conclusion}

\sys investigates how private workspace changes, shared immutable metadata,
and finalized object layouts can be organized under one file-system design.
Delta-based views preserve independent changes, packed snapshots provide
directory-oriented lower access, and the backend interface makes encoding
and physical verification geometry explicit. The source audit establishes
concrete components and identifies the connections and lifecycle support
that still require work. The planned evaluation will determine when the
design reduces total workspace and storage cost, how much of that effect
survives complete agent execution, and where existing approaches remain
preferable. No quantitative performance conclusion is drawn in this draft.
